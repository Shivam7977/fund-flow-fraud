01 · Follow The Money

Fraud doesn't always move straight.

Suspicious activity reveals itself through distinct geometric shapes and multi-hop routing topologies between accounts.

Topology Pattern A

Round-Trip Flows

Money leaves an account and returns after intermediary routing through multiple shell nodes to obscure the original source.

Topology Pattern B

Threshold Structuring

Repeated transactions executed precisely below mandatory regulatory reporting thresholds to evade flagging.

Topology Pattern C

Fan-Out Distribution

A single source account rapidly disburses capital across numerous destination accounts simultaneously.

Topology Pattern D

Fan-In Aggregation

Multiple feeder accounts aggregate capital into a single destination account before immediate secondary withdrawal.
